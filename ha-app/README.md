# SimpleInvoice Manager – Home Assistant App

Write, manage and track invoices from your phone – made for tradespeople (Handwerker).

- Invoices as PDF in German, Polish and English, in the style of classic printed invoices
- Customers, reusable work and material positions, VAT or Kleinunternehmer (§ 19 UStG)
- Gap-free invoice numbers, open / overdue / paid at a glance
- Runs in the Home Assistant sidebar (Ingress) and uses the official MariaDB app
